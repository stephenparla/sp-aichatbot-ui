THE PLATFORM

Make multi-tenant inference
easier to operate.

As AI becomes part of your product, every customer adds requirements around access, performance, capacity, governance, and cost. bCompute brings the operational controls for multi-tenant inference into a consistent management experience—without asking you to replace the inference engines and infrastructure you already use.

01 / ISOLATION◫

Tenant identity & policy

Apply tenant-aware access rules, model permissions, and usage limits consistently across your inference deployments.

02 / ROUTING⌁

Intelligent routing

Direct requests to eligible, healthy inference deployments based on configured model access, deployment status, and capacity signals.

03 / ADAPTERS⧉

Model & adapter operations

Coordinate model and adapter availability across supported runtimes, with visibility into versions, readiness, and deployment state.

04 / VISIBILITY⌗

Usage & cost visibility

Attribute inference usage to the appropriate tenant and application, and give engineering and platform teams a clearer view of operational consumption.

Initial focus: B2B SaaS companies that operate AI features for multiple business customers and need clearer tenant controls, deployment flexibility, and cost attribution. Capabilities are being validated with design partners. Exact integrations, isolation profiles, and supported deployment patterns will be confirmed during the product development process.

A HYBRID OPERATING MODEL

Central visibility.
Customer-local execution.

The architecture separates management from the live inference request path. A central management experience coordinates configuration, tenant policy, model and deployment metadata, and operational visibility. A customer-local data plane handles application traffic, tenant context, local policy checks, routing, and inference execution close to the customer’s workloads.

  • ✓ Central management for tenant configuration, policy, deployment state, and usage
  • ✓ Customer-local gateway and runtime for request handling and inference
  • ✓ Existing inference engines and GPU orchestration remain responsible for execution
  • ✓ Flexible deployment across cloud, customer-managed, and on-prem environments

Conceptual architecture for design discussion. The central management plane is not intended to sit in the synchronous inference path. Customer-local services are designed to continue operating from their last valid configuration during temporary management connectivity interruptions, subject to customer-defined policy and validity settings.

ISOLATION, CONTROL & TRUST

Built around
customer control.

Enterprise teams need predictable boundaries between customers, workloads, and administrative access. bCompute is being designed to make tenant identity, policy decisions, and deployment ownership explicit, with deployment choices that can reflect each customer’s risk and operating requirements.

01

Security-domain boundaries

Support configurable isolation profiles, from logically separated tenant policies on shared capacity to dedicated runtime deployments where stronger separation is required.

02

Customer-controlled data plane

Keep request handling and inference execution in the agreed customer environment; make external model processing and data movement explicit deployment decisions.

03

Least-privilege operations

Use scoped identities, least-privilege administration, controlled configuration changes, and operational audit records.

04

Engine-native execution

Integrate with established inference engines and infrastructure schedulers. bCompute focuses on tenant-aware operations and coordination rather than replacing the underlying GPU scheduler or inference engine.
